\section*{Syllabus}

    \subsection*{Unit 5: Fundamentals of data visualization}
        Principles and use cases for categorical and continuous data plots, including column, line, pie, bar, area and scatter plots; customizing chart elements (axes, scales, legends, and labels); fine-tuning aesthetics for clarity and technical accuracy; advanced plotting techniques: 3D surface plots, contour mapping and multi-layered/multipanel figures.
